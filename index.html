10-Second Habit Setup

Put ZERO Right Next to Your Shopping Apps

On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share icon at the bottom of your screen and tap "Add to Home Screen".

On Android (Chrome): Tap the Three Dots (⋮) at the top right and tap "Add to Home screen".

Whenever you tap the pink Z icon on your phone, it launches your search and jumps straight into your native Amazon, eBay, or AliExpress app!
