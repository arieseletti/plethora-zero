The "Tap ZERO First" Habit

Make ZERO Your Doorway to Shopping

Pinning ZERO doesn't run in the background—it puts the pink Z icon right where your shopping apps live. Whenever you think Amazon, eBay, or AliExpress, tap ZERO first, and we'll open your store with relief tracking active.

On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share icon at the bottom of your screen, then tap "Add to Home Screen".

On Android (Chrome): Tap the Three Dots (⋮) at the top right, then tap "Add to Home screen".
