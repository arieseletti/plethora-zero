Effective AmazonSmile Alternatives for Everyday Giving in 2023

As consumers increasingly embrace online shopping, the desire to give back has also grown. While AmazonSmile has been a popular option for charitable giving, there are several effective alternatives available in 2023 that allow you to support your favorite causes without additional effort. Here’s a comprehensive guide to some of the best options for everyday giving.

1. PayPal Giving Fund

The PayPal Giving Fund allows users to donate to a broad range of charities with each transaction. When you make a purchase through PayPal, you can choose to round up your total or make a direct donation.

2. eBay for Charity

eBay offers a charity program where sellers can donate a portion of their sales to a charity of their choice. Buyers can also choose to add a donation to their purchase, making it easy to support causes while shopping for unique items.

3. Goodshop

Goodshop partners with various online stores to offer coupons that donate a percentage of your purchase to a charity of your choice. This means you can save money while supporting your chosen cause.

4. Giving Assistant

Giving Assistant works similarly to Goodshop, providing cash back for your purchases and donating a percentage to a charity. You can select the charity you want to support each time you shop.
